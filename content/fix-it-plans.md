## plan fixit-stay-closed

cover_fixes: Front shoulder flying open, power leaking toward the foul line, and rollovers on the pull side
symptom_1: Rolling over to the pull side
symptom_2: Weak pop-ups and grounders
symptom_3: Finishing your swing falling toward the foul line

### problem
You want to hit the ball hard, so you try to rotate as much as you can. Your front shoulder flies open, your power leaks out toward the foul line, and you end up getting around the baseball: rollovers, weak pulled grounders, and pop-ups.

### fix
Rotate as fast and hard as you can, but keep that front shoulder closed so your power and your balance stay over the plate. The key to every drill in this plan: keep your eyes and your chest behind contact.

### fixed
- You drive the ball with backspin to the opposite field.
- You consistently fall over the plate after you swing, not out toward the line.
- You stay inside the baseball longer.

## plan fixit-hit-the-brakes
resource_slug: fixit-hit-the-brakes
pdf_filename: LCB_FixIt_Hit_the_Brakes.pdf
title: Hit the Brakes: Stop Drifting, Stay Stacked
subtitle: 3-Week Fix-It Plan
price_label: 3 weeks | $19
button_label: Get Hit the Brakes - $19
cover_fixes: Drifting in your load, losing stack at contact, and hitting the ball softer than you should
symptom_1: Lunging at the ball
symptom_2: Hitting it soft even when you square it up
symptom_3: Feeling like your swing has no power

### problem
All your power is built in your load. When your hips drift toward the ball as you swing, you lose that power. Your hips drag, your barrel drags through the zone, and you hit the ball softer than you should.

### fix
Think about stopping hard on the brakes. The harder you hit the brakes in a car, the harder it stops. The harder your body stops, the harder your barrel comes through. Turn behind your front foot and stay stacked at contact: head over back shoulder, back shoulder over back knee.

### fixed
- You're stacked at contact: head over back shoulder, back shoulder over back knee.
- You're driving the ball with authority, especially the other way.
